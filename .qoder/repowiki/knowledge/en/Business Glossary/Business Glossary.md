### Defense Readiness Score™ (DRS)
- Definition：Composite scoring system (0-100) measuring student preparedness for oral defenses across five weighted dimensions: Technical Depth (30%), Communication (25%), Coverage (20%), Confidence (15%), and Structure (10%). Benchmarked against peer groups and validated against actual viva performance.
- Aliases：DRS、defense readiness score

### VivAI
- Definition：Brand name for the AI-powered defense-readiness platform targeting Indian engineering students. Positioned as the first 'Defense Readiness Platform' category creator, differentiating from generic interview prep tools by focusing specifically on oral defense preparation for academic projects.
- Aliases：VivAI platform、VivAI platform

### Code-Aware Viva
- Definition：Advanced viva mode where students upload their actual project code (ZIP/GitHub), which is processed temporarily to generate contextually relevant questions based on the specific implementation details, architecture, and technical decisions made.
- Aliases：code-aware viva、project-native viva

### DPDP Act Compliance
- Definition：Data protection compliance framework following India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, requiring consent flows for minors under 18, data minimization principles, right to erasure mechanisms, and grievance officer designation.
- Aliases：DPDP compliance、data privacy compliance

### Institution Lite/Pro
- Definition：B2B pricing tiers for colleges: Institution Lite (₹1-3L/year, 500 seats, basic analytics) and Institution Pro (₹5-10L/year, unlimited seats, full feature access including code-aware viva and team collaboration). Targeted at Tier-2/3 engineering colleges focused on placement metrics.
- Aliases：institution pricing、college pricing

### Readiness Benchmarks
- Definition：Anonymized comparative metrics showing student performance relative to peer groups (e.g., 'top 15% of Tier-2 CSE departments'), built from aggregated session data to validate DRS effectiveness and guide improvement strategies.
- Aliases：benchmarks、peer comparison

### Ephemeral Processing
- Definition：Security architecture where student code is uploaded encrypted, processed in memory only during active sessions, and automatically garbage-collected within 7 days. Only anonymized topic coverage percentages persist in reports, never raw source code.
- Aliases：ephemeral code processing、memory-only processing

### NBA/NAAC Alignment
- Definition：Mapping of platform features to National Board of Accreditation criteria (Student Performance, Teaching-Learning Process) to justify institutional procurement budgets and accreditation preparation efforts.
- Aliases：accreditation alignment、NBA criteria
